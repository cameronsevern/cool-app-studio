# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in App Screenshot Forge to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.
Be respectful, assume good intent while responding to actual impact, give useful
technical feedback, and make room for people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's
private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, reviews, project chat,
events, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other content
that violates this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected participants.

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through a private contact method listed
on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue for sensitive
conduct reports. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
